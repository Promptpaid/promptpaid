FAQ
------------------------------------------------------------------
Eyebrow: Questions

Q: What is PromptPaid?
A: An AI training platform. Members hold structured conversations with AI
models and get paid for that work. AI buyers pay for the resulting training
data.

Q: How much can I earn?
A: At full daily activity, a Rookie member can earn up to ₦15,000 a month —
5 paid conversations a day at ₦100 each. It's an upper bound, not a
guarantee: you earn for the days you work.

Q: When can I withdraw for the first time?
A: No earlier than 14 days after you activate, and only once you reach the
₦8,000 withdrawal threshold. The 14-day minimum applies regardless of your
balance.

Q: Are there any fees?
A: Yes. There is a ₦1,000 verification fee charged on every withdrawal.
[LAWYER]

Q: Is there a cost to join the waitlist?
A: No. Joining the waitlist is free. The Rookie tier costs ₦2,000 when you
activate. [CONFIRM: one-time or recurring?]

Q: Which countries and languages are supported?
A: Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana and Kenya. Members can work in English,

Q: When do I start?
A: We're opening access in batches. Join the waitlist and watch your email —
